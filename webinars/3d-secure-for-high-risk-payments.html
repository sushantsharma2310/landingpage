3D-Secure for high-risk payments: Essential strategies for gateways, processors & PayFacaaS

Payment gateways, processors, and PayFac-as-a-Service (PayFacaaS) platforms that serve high-risk merchants face a disproportionate amount of chargebacks, customer drop-off, and support tickets—putting revenue and reputation at risk.

In our exclusive webinar, Shane Curran (Evervault CEO) will share technical expertise and proven best practices to help you build frictionless and effective 3D-Secure experiences that protect payments and maximize conversions.

If you serve high-risk merchants and want to reduce fraud, minimize chargebacks, and build stronger merchant relationships—all while minimizing engineering effort—this webinar is designed for you.

What you’ll learn:

  • Why high-risk merchants impact platform profits and how to address it
  • How to implement frictionless 3D Secure with smart step-up authentication
  • Strategies to reduce chargebacks, boost approvals, and improve merchant satisfaction
  • Developer-friendly tools for fast, effective 3DS integration and support reduction
