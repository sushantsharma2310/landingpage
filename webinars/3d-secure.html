A practical guide to how to implement and optimize 3D-Secure

Join Shane Curran, CEO of Evervault, and Josh Gordon, Product Manager at Stitch, as they unpack the world of 3D-Secure.

They’ll explore how you can use 3D-Secure to decrease fraud rates, chargebacks and optimize your global payment setup, while also sharing practical tips on integrating 3D-Secure into your payment flow. Together, these global payment experts will give you a practical understanding of 3D-Secure, and how to efficiently implement it.

If you're looking to leverage 3D-Secure to improve your payment security, for you and your customers, this session is for you!

What you’ll learn:

  • How does 3DS work under the hood?
  • How do the regulatory guidelines differ between the US vs. EU
  • Why (and when) you should leverage 3DS to reduce fraud rates
  • How to navigate using standalone 3DS vs. 3DS with your payment processor
