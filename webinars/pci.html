A practical guide to PCI script security and 6.4.3 and 11.6.1 compliance

Starting March 31, 2025 all organizations that accept card payments need to comply with enhanced script security regulations, 6.4.3 and 11.6.1 of PCI DSS 4.0.

In our upcoming webinar, Shane Curran (Evervault CEO) and John Hetherton (Evervault Head of Compliance) will cut through the noise of the upcoming script security requirements and run through a step-by-step guide to achieving compliance.

If you need to understand how to protect your payment pages and manage browser scripts in compliance with requirements 6.4.3 and 11.6.1 of PCI DSS 4.0, this webinar is for you.

What we’ll cover:

  • What is 6.4.3 and 11.6.1 of PCI DSS 4.0? And what are its implications to your payment page?
  • How to build a compliant payment page
  • Practical challenges with modern architectures
  • Security challenges
  • Effective strategies and modern tooling
