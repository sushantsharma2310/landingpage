Cookie Policy

Legal

Last updated / April 15 / 2025

This cookie policy describes how Evervault (“Evervault”) uses “cookies” and other similar technologies, in connection with our Site and Services. For more information on Evervault’s privacy practices, please see our Privacy Policy.

About cookies

A cookie is a small piece of information stored on your computer via the web browser you use. Cookies are used to personalize your web experience with a website. They help with things like understanding how you use a website, remembering your login details, and storing site preferences.

How Evervault uses cookies

Evervault uses two types of cookies: first and third-party cookies. First party cookies are set and read by us when you use Evervault. Third party cookies are set by companies other than Evervault and collect information about you which may be sent to a third party. Evervault uses third party cookies for site analytics and marketing purposes.

Evervault may use cookies for:

Remembering Preferences. To remember your preferences and to recognize you when you return to Evervault.

Analytics. To help us improve your experience, Evervault uses services like Google Analytics, Plausible, Swan, Vector, Reddit, HubSpot, Segment and LinkedIn to understand how people use our website — such as which pages are visited and which links are clicked.

Communications. We use Plain as a channel for communications with our customers. In order to remember whether you had visited before and to keep track of existing conversations Plain uses a cookie.

Opting out

You can simply select to Reject Cookies when you arrive on the Evervault.com website, after which no cookie data will be saved to your local machine.

Updates

Evervault may revise this Cookie Policy at any time by amending this page. Please check it from time to time, and take note of any changes.
