How it works

Page monitoring and alerting in minutes

Evervault Page Protection helps protect companies from sophisticated card data breaches, while directly addressing requirements 6.4.3 and 11.6.1 in PCI DSS Version 4.0.

With AI-driven monitoring and alerting, you'll detect potentially malicious script and security header changes before they impact your customers.

Track and manage script inventory

Page Protection will create an audit-ready inventory of all scripts loaded on your page. You can easily authorize and justify each script (requirement 6.4.3) and generate audit reports on demand to demonstrate PCI DSS 4.0 compliance.
